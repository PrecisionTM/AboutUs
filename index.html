Built on the Belief That Healthcare
Should Feel Personal Again.

As Owner and CEO, Carlie Cannon-Rius built Precision Telemed on the belief that modern healthcare should be accessible, supportive, and centered around the patient experience. After seeing how difficult it can be for people to navigate traditional healthcare systems, she set out to create a platform where patients could receive high-quality medical care, expert guidance, and genuine support all in one place.

Carlie launched Precision Telemed with a simple vision: healthcare should feel personal again. Too often, patients were left navigating complex health challenges without the support, education, or guidance they deserved. Precision Telemed was built to change that — starting with a US-based customer service team of trained professionals who answer the phone, know your name, and genuinely care about your outcome.

Her goal was to create a platform where patients receive safe, clinically supervised treatments supported by lifestyle, nutrition, and long-term wellness strategies — and where every question gets answered by a real person, based in the United States. Individualized care that looks at the whole person, not just the symptom.
